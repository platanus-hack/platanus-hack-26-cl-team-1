# Lockout test: prompts for the ElevenLabs app (Image & Video)

Images: Nano Banana Pro, 16:9. Make k2 first, then k3 and m1 with k2 as the reference image.

## k2_isolated

Photorealistic still from a fixed security camera on a tripod, wide shot, 16:9, inside a copper mine processing plant at night, harsh industrial sodium and LED lighting, dust in the air. On the left, mounted on a steel pillar at chest height, a large grey electrical isolation switch box with a big red rotary handle and a yellow sign 'PUNTO DE BLOQUEO'. On the right, a long rubber conveyor belt carrying crushed ore, with a large rock jammed between the belt and a roller. A single mine worker in an orange high-visibility jacket, white hard hat, safety glasses and grey work gloves. Everything in sharp focus, no text overlays, no watermark. The conveyor belt is stopped. The red handle of the isolation switch is turned to OFF (horizontal). Nothing hangs from the handle. The worker stands next to the switch box, facing it, holding a red safety padlock with a red tag in his right hand.

## k3_locked

Same scene, same camera, same worker, same lighting. A red safety padlock now hangs clearly from the red handle of the isolation switch, with a large red 'PELIGRO NO OPERAR' tag attached to it. The worker has stepped back half a meter from the switch box, hands empty.

## m1_hand_in_unlocked

Same scene, same camera, same worker, same lighting. Nothing hangs from the red handle of the isolation switch: no padlock, no tag. The worker has walked to the conveyor and is reaching with both gloved hands toward the jammed rock between the belt and the roller.

Videos: Veo 3.1 Fast, 8 s, 16:9, 720p, no audio, start frame and end frame as below.

## c_lock: start k2_isolated, end k3_locked

The worker puts the red padlock through the hole in the red handle of the isolation switch, snaps it shut, hangs the red tag on it, then steps back. Fixed camera, no camera movement, realistic motion.

## c_mistake_hand_in: start k2_isolated, end m1_hand_in_unlocked

The worker puts the padlock in his pocket, walks away from the isolation switch without locking it and reaches toward the jammed rock on the conveyor belt with both hands. Fixed camera, no camera movement, realistic motion.
